01 SYS-001
Summer 2026 SYS QNT
Nonlinear control

Stabilising a speculative bubble

State-feedback control of a market where traders switch strategies.

For 40.018 Heuristics & Systems Theory, our team of three took the Schmitt, Tramontana & Westerhoff (2020) market model, where traders flip between chasing trends and betting on fair value depending on which paid off last period, and asked whether a regulator using full state feedback could beat the paper's fixed price rules.

I led the linearisation, Lyapunov stability, controllability and observability analysis, and the controller and observer design. At fair value the model is only rank-2 controllable, so we placed poles on the controllable subsystem. On the true nonlinear model it held for every bubble size we tested, and only failed when trader behaviour drifted, which re-tuning fixed.

04 ML-004
Credit Bureau SG ML
Synthetic data

Credit data that isn't real

A synthetic data generator for Credit Bureau Singapore.

An industry project reporting to DBS, OCBC and UOB. Real credit data can't be used for testing or client demos, so our team of five built a generator that mimics it.

The tool clusters banks' time series with K-medoids (k chosen by silhouette score), synthesises each cluster with seven methods (ARIMA, Holt-Winters, Prophet, Copula, Beta, Gaussian and CTGAN), scores every method for fidelity and recommends the best. I led the CTGAN pipeline and designed the dual-view (Customer/Agency) dashboard.

80.7/100 best fidelity: Copula, account-balance series
