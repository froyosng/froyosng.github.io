01 / Quant research2026

Signals that survive noise

Alpha research for WorldQuant IQC and AlphaNova.

On WorldQuant's BRAIN platform, I built (with a partner) a delay-0 signal combining volume dynamics with news sentiment, and a delay-1 growth-at-a-reasonable-price valuation signal. Both passed BRAIN's full in-sample validation suite, reaching IQC Stage 2.

For AlphaNova, I built walk-forward, Ridge-based cross-sectional signals ranking 20 assets per timestamp. Two of them made the prize-eligible “Quality set”.

02 / Predictive modelling2026

The discrete choice machine

Predicting which car safety-feature bundle people choose.

Analytics Edge Data Competition on Kaggle. I modelled the choice with conditional and mixed logit, then added price-factor identification, task-fatigue effects and an XGBoost blend. Along the way I diagnosed and fixed a rank-deficient design matrix.

04 / Computer visionDTI 2025

A camera that scares birds

CV detection wired to a physical deterrent.

A computer vision-triggered bird deterrent for the SUTD canteen. When a mynah is detected, a hawk decoy on a ceiling-mounted rail swoops towards it and plays a hawk call to scare it away. I integrated the detection logic with the physical actuation.
