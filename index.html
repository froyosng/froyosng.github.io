Volume–Sentiment Alpha — a delay-0 signal combining trading volume dynamics with news sentiment data.

GARP-Style Valuation Alpha — a delay-1 signal built on a growth-at-a-reasonable-price framework.

Both signals passed BRAIN's full in-sample validation suite (Sharpe, Fitness, turnover, and sub-universe stability checks), qualifying them for submission under IQC rules.
