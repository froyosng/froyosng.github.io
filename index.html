01 OPT-001
Summer 2026 OPT
Multi-objective optimisation

$200M, 46 countries, one trade-off

Allocating HIV funding when impact and fairness disagree.

−33%inequity at the knee point, keeping 98.6% of greedy's DALYs averted

After cuts to US foreign aid, suppose a funder commits US$200M to HIV programmes across 46 African countries and four interventions: ARV drugs, supply chains, workforce training and drug-resistance surveillance. Maximising DALYs averted and minimising inequity between countries pull in different directions, across 184 decision variables in a non-convex model.

Our team of three compared a greedy heuristic, simulated annealing, NSGA-II and a reduced Gurobi benchmark. I implemented the NSGA-II pipeline (encoding, non-dominated sorting, crowding distance, budget-preserving crossover and mutation, repair, knee-point selection) plus the benchmark comparison and all plots.

Python · NSGA-II · Simulated annealing · Gurobi

02 QNT-002
Edition 2026 QNT
Signal research

Signals that survive noise

Alpha research for WorldQuant IQC and AlphaNova.

2.21top in-sample Sharpe (Fitness 2.30), IQC Gold tier

For WorldQuant's IQC, I built (with a partner) market-neutral equity alphas across price, fundamental and sentiment signals, reaching Gold tier and Stage 2 (top 22 in Singapore, #1,376 globally). To search faster I wrote a Python BRAIN API pipeline with an expression canonicaliser, template-based generation and SQLite experiment tracking.

For AlphaNova, I built walk-forward Ridge ranking signals (z-scores, rank velocity) for 20 assets per timestamp; two passed the overfitting and originality checks for the prize-eligible Quality set. Of 6,000+ participants, fewer than 1,000 signals passed initial checks and only 160 survived the overfitting, look-ahead and correlation filters. Along the way I caught a silent feature–target misalignment caused by MultiIndex column ordering.

Python · BRAIN API · SQLite · Ridge

04 ML-004
Credit Bureau SG ML
Synthetic data

Credit data that isn't real

A synthetic data generator for Credit Bureau Singapore.

80.7/100 best fidelity: Copula, account-balance series

An industry project reporting to DBS, OCBC and UOB. Real credit data can't be used for testing or client demos, so our team of five built a generator that mimics it.

The tool clusters banks' time series with K-medoids (k chosen by silhouette score), synthesises each cluster with seven methods (ARIMA, Holt-Winters, Prophet, Copula, Beta, Gaussian and CTGAN), scores every method for fidelity and recommends the best. I led the CTGAN pipeline and designed the dual-view (Customer/Agency) dashboard.

Python · Streamlit · CTGAN · K-medoids

  • Psych

    Algorithm aversion in financial decisions

    A 2 × 2 within-subjects study (AI robo-advisor vs. human advisor × stakes of up to 5% vs. 40%) with 42 participants, in a team of five. High stakes cut both willingness to delegate and the advice's influence, but equally for AI and human advisors, with no moderation by experience, task subjectivity or trust. Big stakes make people rely less on advice in general, not on algorithms specifically.
